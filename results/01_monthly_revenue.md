# 01. Monthly revenue, month-over-month change and running totals

**Question:** How much did completed repairs bring in each month, how did that compare with the month before, and what is the running total for the year and since the data starts?

**Techniques:** CTEs, LAG() to read the previous month's row, SUM() OVER with a window frame for running totals, NULLIF so a zero month can't divide by zero.

**Notes:** Revenue is the final_price of completed bookings (it includes the travel fee), counted in the month the job was completed.
