# 09. Average rating by technician (at least 30 reviews)

**Question:** Which technicians get the best reviews? A technician only makes the table with 30 or more reviews, so a handful of five-star reviews can't put someone at the top.

**Techniques:** joins across three tables, GROUP BY with HAVING to filter whole groups, and RANK() over an aggregate.

**Notes:** WHERE filters rows before grouping; HAVING filters the groups after it. Window functions run after HAVING, so the rank only compares the technicians who qualify.
