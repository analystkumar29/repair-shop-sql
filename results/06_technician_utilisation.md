# 06. Technician utilisation and jobs per working day

**Question:** How many jobs does each technician complete on a day they work, how often do they have a day with no job at all, and what share of their shift hours goes on hands-on repair work?

**Techniques:** a LEFT JOIN from the shifts table so days without a job still count, join conditions kept in ON (not WHERE) so the LEFT JOIN isn't undone, and aggregating in two steps: per shift, then per technician.

**Notes:** Hands-on time uses each service's typical duration. Travel between jobs isn't tracked, so utilisation here means repair time only.
