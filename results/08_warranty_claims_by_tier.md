# 08. Warranty claim rate by price tier

**Question:** Do cheaper tiers come back under warranty more often, and how does that compare with what each tier costs?

**Techniques:** CTEs, a LEFT JOIN so jobs without a claim still count, and a fixed observation window so every tier is compared on the same terms.

**Notes:** Warranty length differs by tier (3, 6 or 12 months), so simply counting all claims would compare different amounts of time. Instead this counts claims made within 90 days of the repair, and only for jobs completed at least 90 days before the data ends, so every job had the full 90 days.
