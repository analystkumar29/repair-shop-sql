# 05. Repeat customers and monthly cohort retention

**Question:** What share of customers come back for another repair, and how soon? Customers are grouped into cohorts by the month of their first completed repair.

**Techniques:** chained CTEs, MIN() to find each customer's first and second repair, a LEFT JOIN that keeps customers who never returned, conditional counts, and a CASE that returns NULL when a cohort is too young to measure.

**Notes:** returned_so_far_pct is the plain repeat rate, so older cohorts score higher simply because they've had longer. The fixed windows fix that: a cohort's "within 90 days" figure only appears once every customer in it has had 90 days to come back, and stays NULL until then rather than showing a misleadingly low number. Duplicate customer records (query 10) make all of these rates slightly understated.
