# 03. Top three device models in each city

**Question:** In each service area, which device models do we repair most often? That decides which parts ride along in the van on a route day.

**Techniques:** GROUP BY, RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY ...) to rank models within each city, a window SUM for the city's total, and filtering on the rank in the outer query (WHERE runs before window functions, so it can't filter on them in the same SELECT).

**Notes:** RANK() gives tied models the same rank, so a city can list more than three models when there's a tie for third place. ROW_NUMBER() would always keep exactly three and break the tie arbitrarily.
