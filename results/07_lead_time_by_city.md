# 07. Booking lead time by city

**Question:** How long do customers wait between booking and their appointment in each area? The median shows the typical wait; the 90th percentile shows the long tail that an average would hide.

**Techniques:** interval arithmetic, PERCENTILE_CONT(...) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY ...), an ordered-set aggregate for medians and percentiles, and a conditional count.

**Notes:** Uses confirmed bookings, whose scheduled_for is the agreed appointment. The outer areas are only visited on route days (service_days), which is why their waits are longer.
