# 02. Revenue by service and price tier

**Question:** Which repairs bring in the most money, and how does each one's revenue split across the Value, Recommended and Premium tiers?

**Techniques:** joins to lookup tables, conditional aggregation (a CASE inside SUM) to turn the tiers into columns, SUM(SUM(...)) OVER () for each service's share of all revenue.

**Notes:** The inner SUM is the per-service total from GROUP BY; the outer SUM() OVER () adds those totals across every row of the result, which gives the grand total without a second query.
